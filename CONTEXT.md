# SaltAndLight

SaltAndLight helps parents find childcare through transparent safety, experience, community, and household-alignment signals.

## Language

**Parent**:
A person seeking and responsible for selecting childcare for a child.
_Avoid_: Customer, client

**Sitter**:
A childcare provider whose profile can be considered by a parent.
_Avoid_: Candidate, worker

**Urgent Replacement Request**:
A parent's time-sensitive search for a sitter after planned childcare becomes unavailable.
_Avoid_: Emergency booking

**Trust Profile**:
The collection of visible, attributable signals a parent uses to evaluate a sitter, including safety checks, training, experience, reviews, and community connections. It supports a parent's decision but does not guarantee trustworthiness.
_Avoid_: Trust score, trusted badge

**Verified Childcare Experience**:
Childcare work history supported by platform records or confirmed references.
_Avoid_: Business experience

**Community Connection**:
A visible relationship between a parent and sitter through a church or another real-world community. A connection is not an endorsement unless explicitly identified as one.
_Avoid_: Church-approved

**Household Preferences**:
A parent's stated expectations for routines, boundaries, and values while a sitter cares for their child.
_Avoid_: Values score, culture fit
